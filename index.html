About

I’m Sushanth, a developer focused on building interactive simulations and tools that make complex concepts easier to understand. I work on projects that simplify how people learn and interact with technical concepts, especially in areas where theory often feels abstract or difficult to visualize. Currently, I’m building tools that model real-world behaviors, simulate long-term outcomes of everyday actions, and create interactive visualizations that make system-level concepts more intuitive. I enjoy turning ideas into practical tools and continuously experimenting with new concepts to improve both my skills and the way people learn through technology.
